Field Note: Comms Backup Procedure

This note documents the manual fallback procedure for the bridge comms relay when the primary uplink drops for more than five minutes.

Trigger conditions

  • Primary uplink heartbeat missed three times consecutively
  • Manual override called by the duty officer

Steps

  1. Switch relay selector to SECONDARY
  2. Confirm secondary handshake within 30 seconds
  3. Log the failover event with timestamp and operator initials

Escalation table

Minutes downAction
0-5Monitor only
5-15Switch to secondary relay
15+Page on-call engineer
