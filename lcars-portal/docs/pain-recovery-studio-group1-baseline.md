# Pain Recovery Studio: Group 1 baseline

Status: Group 1 baseline in progress
Purpose: preserve all pre-Group 1 capability commitments before Group 2 begins.

## Delivered in the baseline

- Staged daily flow: morning, plan, active, evening and safety hold.
- Deterministic Green, Amber and Red day logic.
- Current-step-only presentation.
- Guide response with concrete next action, choices and practice.
- Safety boundary and clinician authority language.
- Global curated pain resources across Australia, UK, Canada, US, New Zealand and international sources.
- Direct routes for Guide Me, overwhelmed support, resources, strengths and weekly review.
- Unified “What do you need right now?” launcher.
- “None of these” escape route.
- Strength selections saved into the learning loop.
- Overwhelmed selections submitted to the current session.
- Strength context passed into the guide.
- Richer evening learning choices.
- Weekly review with neutral empty state, accessible labels, validation and save feedback.
- Main Studio loading timeout, retry state and direct overwhelmed fallback.
- Audio/listen support on the overwhelmed route.
- Resource “Start here” recommendation and need-based shortcuts.

## Pre-Group 1 commitments carried forward

These remain part of the baseline contract and must not be dropped:

- Adaptive overwhelmed pathways for fatigue, overload, isolation, symptom concern and inability to act.
- Resilience model covering protective factors, valued roles, confidence, participation and recovery cost.
- Progress measures beyond pain intensity.
- Audio guidance across low-energy practices, not only overwhelmed support.
- Resource filtering by need, region, language, cost and eligibility.
- MBSR cross-navigation and shared visual language.
- Cross-day continuity for yesterday’s cost, unresolved choices, strengths and repeat plans.
- Richer evening learning loop.
- VoiceOver, keyboard and iPhone-size validation.
- Slow-network and retry testing.
- Clinician review of direct-route safety wording.
- Resource ownership, source dates and review dates.
- Minimal usability analytics with health-data minimisation.
- Testing normal days, overwhelmed days, missed days and return-after-gap journeys.

## Baseline gate before Group 2

Group 1 is not complete until:

1. The corrected branch builds successfully.
2. Production is not promoted until the build passes.
3. Direct-route saves return a clear success or bounded fallback.
4. Strengths appear in Today and influence the next guide response.
5. The main Studio and direct routes pass the low-energy patient walkthrough.
6. Accessibility labels and keyboard/VoiceOver behaviour are checked.
7. Safety wording is explicitly marked interim where clinician review is still pending.
8. The pre-Group 1 commitments above remain visible in the product backlog.

## Safety boundary

The Studio does not diagnose, judge danger, prescribe exercise or medication, or replace clinician advice. Peter remains the exercise authority. New, sudden, progressive or concerning symptoms must route to the agreed safety boundary.
