## Quick Navigation

**To find everything about a specialist:** 
1. Start with `specialists/[core-crew or future-crew]/[Specialist-Name].md`
2. Cross-reference `SPECIALIST-INVENTORY.md` and `SPECIALIST-ROLE-CATALOGUE.md` for role and responsibilities
3. Check `core/crew/[specialist-folder]/` for operational details and governance
4. Review `specialists/knowledge-packs/[Specialist-Name]-Knowledge.md` for expertise
5. See associated frameworks in `specialists/knowledge-packs/` for operational guidance
6. Check `specialists/activation-plans/[Specialist-Name]-Activation.md` for commissioning status
