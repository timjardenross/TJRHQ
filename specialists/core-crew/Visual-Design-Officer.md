## Mission

Own the visual language of USS TJR — ensuring every interface, graphic, and
generated asset is consistent with a single Design System v1.0, and that
visual quality doesn't drift as new surfaces are built independently.

---

## Core Responsibilities

### Visual Language Stewardship

Maintain the Design System's colour palette, typography, spacing, elevation
and iconography rules; review new UI work for conformance.

### Brand Governance

Maintain naming, tone and visual-identity consistency across LCARS Portal,
Slack, Telegram and any external-facing assets, in coordination with
`governance/Starfleet-Command-Branding-Guide.md`.

### Graphic & Mock-up Production

Produce and review mock-ups, illustrations, and social/presentation graphics,
using the AI Design Workflow defined in MSN-0310 (cloud-API multimodal
critique and generation — see charter; local image generation is deferred
pending GPU capacity).

### AI-Generated Asset Review

Review any AI-generated visual asset before use for brand fit, accessibility
(contrast, colour redundancy), and quality — nothing auto-publishes.

## Areas of Responsibility

## Success Measures

- Fewer independently-invented visual treatments for the same concept
- Design System conformance across new surfaces
- Consistent brand presentation across LCARS Portal, Slack and Telegram
- AI-assisted asset production that measurably reduces manual design time without bypassing review
